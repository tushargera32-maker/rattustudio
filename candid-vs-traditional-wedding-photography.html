Candid vs Traditional Wedding Photography: Punjab Couples Guide

By RATTU Digital Studio, Jandu Singha Jalandhar · 6 min read · Updated Sept 2026

"Should we choose candid or traditional?" — every couple in Jalandhar asks this question. After watching Instagram reels, candid looks like everything. Then family reminds you that formal family portraits are essential. Both perspectives are valid. Here is an honest breakdown, without marketing exaggeration.

We are RATTU Digital Studio — with over 12 years of experience shooting both styles across Jalandhar, Adampur, Alawalpur, Bhogpur and Jalandhar Cantt. Because we specialise in both disciplines, we can offer genuinely impartial guidance.

Clear definitions, without jargon

CANDID 📸

No poses, no forced smiles. Laughter, tears, emotional parents and joyful friends — whatever unfolds is captured as it happens. The photographer remains unobtrusive. The result is honest emotion that will still move you ten years from now.

TRADITIONAL 🪔

Posed, directed and complete. Family groups, Anand Karaj rituals documented step by step, bridal portraits and attire details. The photographer guides every frame. The result is a complete record of the wedding, perfect for framing and displaying at home.

Advantages and limitations of candid photography

  • Advantage: authentic emotions, the joy of sangeet and haldi, genuine couple chemistry, and frames ready for Instagram reels
  • Advantage: camera-shy couples feel comfortable — with no pressure to pose
  • Limitation: candid-only coverage can miss formal family groups, which elders will certainly notice
  • Limitation: skilled candid specialists are premium and rare — many claim the title, few deliver true artistry

Advantages and limitations of traditional photography

  • Advantage: the entire wedding is documented — Anand Karaj, pheras, vidaai and large family groups are all securely covered
  • Advantage: elders are delighted, with elegant portraits ready for framing and family albums
  • Limitation: excessive posing can be tiring, and couples can appear stiff
  • Limitation: traditional-only coverage can miss a cinematic feel — emotions may appear less vivid

Our recommendation: the 60-40 combination (the Punjab formula)

After more than 1500 Punjabi weddings, our recommendation is clear: 60% candid emotion + 40% traditional coverage creates the perfect album. The practical approach is to work with two specialists — a candid photographer dedicated to the couple, and a traditional photographer covering family and rituals. Plan for sangeet and haldi to be 80% candid, the Anand Karaj 70% traditional, couple portraits split evenly, and vidaai captured entirely candidly — that moment cannot be posed.

Real wedding day example: a wedding in Jalandhar Cantt

Consider a morning Anand Karaj at a gurdwara in Jalandhar Cantt — the traditional photographer documents each laavan step by step while maintaining a respectful distance. Meanwhile, the candid photographer captures a mother's tears and quiet smiles. In the evening, at a Model Town banquet reception, formal family groups are completed efficiently in traditional style within 20 minutes, leaving the rest of the night for candid dance-floor storytelling. A drone adds scale to the baraat entry. This teamwork creates a memorable Punjabi wedding film, a formula that works equally well in Adampur, Bhogpur and every venue across Punjab.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is candid photography more expensive than traditional photography?

It requires fast lenses, full-day attentive presence and extensive moment-selection editing. Skilled candid specialists are rare, which is reflected in premium pricing.

Is candid coverage alone sufficient?

For a Punjabi wedding, usually not. Family groups, Anand Karaj rituals and portraits for elders require traditional coverage. The strongest album blends 60% candid with 40% traditional.

Which style is best for camera-shy couples?

Candid — with no posing pressure. We first help you relax, then capture natural moments. Working with camera-shy couples is our speciality.

Can both styles be included in one package?

Yes. The RATTU Studio team includes specialists in both styles — candid, traditional, film and album can all be included in one package.
