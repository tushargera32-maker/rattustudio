10 Best Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations in Jalandhar & Adampur (2026)

By RATTU Digital Studio, Jandu Singha · 7 min read · Updated Sept 2026

A pre-wedding shoot is the most joyful part of a Punjabi wedding celebration — no family pressure, no demanding schedule. Just the two of you, elegant outfits, and a cinematic mood. The first question is always: which location should you choose? The Jalandhar–Adampur belt offers an exceptional range of settings, when you know where to look.

At RATTU Digital Studio, we have photographed over 500 pre-weddings — from the manicured greens of Jalandhar Cantt to the luxury resorts of Bhogpur. Here is our tested 2026 list, complete with permission details, ideal timing, and outfit recommendations.

01 · ROYAL VIBE

01. Haveli Farmhouses, Adampur Road

Punjabi haveli-style farms featuring arched corridors, open courtyards, and vintage doors. Sherwani and lehenga portraits look truly cinematic here. Permission: Rs. 8,000–15,000. Best time: November to February, morning. Tip: a drone shot over the courtyard is essential.

02 · NATURE CLASSIC

02. Mustard Fields near Adampur & Alawalpur

In January and February, the golden-yellow mustard fields showcase Punjab at its finest. A yellow lehenga or a white gown stands out beautifully against the blooms. Access is usually free or a Rs. 2,000 gesture of appreciation to farmers. Best light: 4–5:30 PM at sunset.

03 · GREEN & CLEAN

03. Jalandhar Cantt Greens

Manicured lawns, tree-lined avenues, and refined colonial character. Perfect for relaxed couple portraits and natural walking reels. Morning access is easy with minimal crowds. Formal wear or pastel outfits photograph best here.

04 · RESORT LUXURY

04. Bhogpur–Kartarpur Resorts

Poolside decks, elegant gazebos, and fairy-lit evenings. Evening gown and suit portraits look exceptional after dark. Permission: Rs. 5,000–12,000. Book one evening plus the following morning — two distinct looks in a single booking.

05 · VILLAGE CHARM

05. Alawalpur Village Homes & Streets

Earthy textures, heritage doors, tractors, and traditional village gathering spaces. Our top choice for a Punjabi folk-inspired song shoot. Style with a kurta-patiala and phulkari look. With warm, respectful communication, locals usually grant permission graciously.

06 · HERITAGE

06. Devi Talab Mandir Complex Periphery & Old City Lanes

Soft morning light combined with heritage architecture. Traditional red and gold bridal attire looks radiant here. Arrive by 6:30 AM to avoid crowds, and choose modest, respectful outfits.

07 · MODERN COUPLE

07. Model Town Cafes & Rooftops, Jalandhar

Ideal for candid coffee-date reels and stylish neon-lit night portraits. A favourite among modern couples. Simply request permission from the cafe and order brunch — arrangements are usually straightforward.

08 · WATER & SKY

08. Canal Banks, Bhogpur Side

Golden-hour reflections, riverside walks, and graceful dupatta-in-motion frames. Water is clearest after monsoon. Always shoot with your team nearby for safety and stay away from the edge.

09 · FILMY SONG

09. Pushpa Gujral Science City & Wonderland Periphery

Expansive modern architecture and vibrant backdrops — energetic settings for pre-wedding song lip-sync sequences. Entry is ticketed, with lighter crowds on weekdays.

10 · OUR SECRET

10. Jandu Singha Sunset Point (Farm Belt)

Our home-ground signature spot — open skies, soaring kites, and an authentic Punjab sunset. No permission required, no crowds. A white and red combination looks striking here. The exact location pin is shared exclusively with RATTU couples.

5 Essential Tips for Your Pre-Wedding Shoot

1. Choose two looks, not three. One traditional and one western or casual. Three outfits consume valuable shooting time and cost you the best light. 2. Wear comfortable footwear. Heels sink in soft fields — always carry elegant flats. 3. Practise lip-sync for song shoots. Listen to your song at least ten times before the shoot day. 4. Combine your makeup trial with the shoot day — it saves cost and ensures a polished look. 5. Check wind conditions on drone days — afternoons in the Adampur belt can be windy, so mornings are ideal.

Cost and Planning — An Honest Breakdown

Single-day, single-location stills with a teaser: Rs. 15,000–35,000. Two-location cinematic song production: Rs. 40,000–80,000, including concept, direction, editing, and location assistance. Location permission charges are additional. Travel beyond 30 km, such as Bhogpur-side resorts, is charged nominally. Our recommendation: schedule your shoot 1–2 months before the wedding, so your album and LED trailer are ready on time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for a pre-wedding shoot in Jalandhar?

October to March is ideal. Mustard fields peak in January and February. In summer, shoot only between 6–8 AM to avoid harsh light.

What is the cost of a pre-wedding shoot?

Single location Rs. 15,000–35,000; cinematic song across two locations Rs. 40,000–80,000. Permission charges are additional. The exact quote depends on concept and locations.

How do location permissions work?

Resorts and farms charge Rs. 5,000–15,000, while parks require only a ticket. We provide complete assistance with permissions and scheduling.

Do you also shoot pre-wedding songs?

Yes — we specialise in concept, locations, lip-sync direction, and release-ready edits. This is the signature expertise of RATTU Studio.

Which natural locations are near Adampur?

Mustard fields, canal banks, village roads, and farmhouses — ideal for sunset and folk-song shoots. Alawalpur offers authentic village charm.
